CASE STUDY

Cinevision: Cillian Murphy Retrospective Key Art & Editorial Poster

Cinematic portrait treatment blending high-contrast split lighting with vintage festival editorial typography.

• EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Creative Brief

The film retrospective needed an iconic centerpiece poster that captured the transformative gravitas of Cillian Murphy's career—bridging historical drama, noir tension, and psychological cinema into one unified visual narrative.

The Design Solution

Created a multi-layered composite utilizing a striking split duotone color palette. Deep shadow undertones are counterbalanced by vivid amber highlights, honoring classic 35mm film grain. The composition leverages strict Swiss typographic alignment, framing the subject with clean festival metadata, bold tracking, and authentic credit typography.

Craft & Execution

  • 🎨 Split Duotone Grading: Hand-crafted curves in Photoshop for cinematic tonal depth.
  • 📐 Swiss Grid Hierarchy: Mathematical layout structuring festival dates, credits, and billing blocks.
  • 🖨️ Print Perfection: Color-managed CMYK separation calibrated for 300 DPI large-format exhibition.
Print Spec: 300 DPI CMYK ⚡

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How fast can a brand identity or poster project be delivered?

Most standard graphic design projects (posters, key art, logo identities, lookbook layouts) are conceptualized, refined with feedback, and fully delivered within 3 to 7 business days.

What file formats and production deliverables do you provide?

You receive full industry-standard deliverables: print-ready 300 DPI CMYK PDFs with bleed marks, lossless vector master files (AI, EPS, SVG), and high-resolution transparent PNG/JPEG assets for digital and web use.

How do revisions work and how do I start a project?

Every package includes structured revision rounds to ensure the final artwork exceeds your expectations. To start, simply tell us "We are ready" on our Instagram page from where you got the link!
