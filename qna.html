Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about our graphic design process, brand identity systems, poster creation, deliverables, and turnaround times.

We specialize in comprehensive visual design: bespoke brand identity systems, logos and wordmarks, cinematic and event posters, custom typography and lettering, editorial and lookbook layouts, apparel graphics, packaging mockups, and high-resolution prepress print preparation.

We work in industry-standard creative software: Adobe Illustrator for vector brandmarks and geometric grids, Adobe Photoshop for complex photo compositing and analog grain grading, Adobe InDesign for multi-page editorial layouts, and Figma for collaborative digital asset systems.

Most focused design projects (single poster designs, key art, logo identities) are conceptualized, refined with your feedback, and fully delivered within 3 to 7 business days. Comprehensive multi-touchpoint brand identity systems typically take 10 to 14 business days.

You receive turnkey, professional production deliverables: print-ready 300 DPI CMYK PDFs with bleed and crop marks, lossless vector source files (AI, EPS, SVG), and high-resolution transparent PNG/JPEG assets optimized for web, social media, and digital presentations.

Yes, 100%. Upon project completion and final signoff, you receive full commercial ownership of all vector source files and final assets. You have complete freedom to print, manufacture, trademark, and distribute the artwork worldwide without any recurring licensing fees.

Every project includes dedicated revision rounds to dial in the concepts, typography, and color grading. Furthermore, every build comes with a 30-day post-delivery warranty — if your printer requests a different color profile, alternate bleed margins, or specific file re-exports during your first month, I provide them promptly at zero extra cost.
